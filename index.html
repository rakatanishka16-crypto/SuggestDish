Frequently asked questions

How is SuggestDish different from restaurant search?

Start with a dish, your budget and your city. We rank matching menu records and link to available ordering options. Paid promotions are clearly labelled and do not increase a dish’s match score.

How are dishes ranked?

Taste and cuisine match, budget fit, available ratings, verification recency and anonymous helpfulness votes contribute to the score. Diet and budget are hard filters. Unknown data adds no invented score.

Privacy and preference controls

Your search preferences and selected location are sent to SuggestDish to find dishes. Preferences may be sent to Google Gemini to interpret your request; locality resolution may use Geoapify. Precise location is requested through browser permission. Saved dishes and optional remembered preferences remain on this device. Location is not included in remembered preferences.

Anonymous recommendation displays and outbound dish-link clicks are counted; these counts are not unique users or orders. Feedback is stored for private moderation. Business contact details and ownership evidence are used for listing review. Keep private access codes private. You can remove saved dishes and forget remembered preferences using the website controls. Email hello@suggestdish.com for other privacy requests.

Paid plans are available when secure checkout is enabled. Buying dish slots does not guarantee placement; any future sponsored placement must be labelled and respect your dietary and budget constraints.
