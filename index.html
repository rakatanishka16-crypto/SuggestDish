About SuggestDish

SuggestDish is a dish discovery website. We match available menu dishes to your preferences; restaurant listings, dish evidence and source coverage can be incomplete.

Help and corrections

Use Report incorrect information on a dish card, or contact @suggestdish.ai on Instagram. Business ownership and menu changes are reviewed before publication.

Privacy and preference controls

Your search preferences and selected location are sent to SuggestDish to find dishes. Preferences may be sent to Google Gemini to interpret your request; locality resolution may use Geoapify. Precise location is requested through browser permission. Saved dishes and optional remembered preferences remain on this device. Location is not included in remembered preferences.

Anonymous recommendation displays and outbound dish-link clicks are counted; these counts are not unique users or orders. Feedback is stored for private moderation. Business contact details and ownership evidence are used for listing review. Keep private access codes private. You can remove saved dishes and forget remembered preferences using the website controls. Contact us on Instagram for other privacy requests.

Paid plans are paused. Buying dish slots does not guarantee placement; any future sponsored placement must be labelled and respect your dietary and budget constraints.
