# Dish-to-outlet research snapshot — 5 October 2026

The 100-dish shortlist is curated; it is not a measured popularity ranking. Each entry points to reviewed menu IDs rather than fuzzy name matches. Regional variants and combinations are described in each dish's note. Separate items do not acquire a fabricated combo price.

The accompanying compressed food-gap snapshot adds 226 outlet profiles and 297 menu entries and enriches 87 existing profiles with brand references. Bikanervala accounts for 97 new profiles and Bikkgane Biryani for 101. Other additions cover regional dishes, momos, Korean food and desserts. Source URLs and retrieval dates accompany each observation.

Branch locator extraction excluded 23 records with unreliable address extraction; these are recorded in `research_scope.excluded_records`. Two published directions destinations had impossible India coordinates and were omitted. The batch retains 192 source map pins. Pins are not independently checked; travel distances are not inferred.

Most evidence comes from official business websites, menus and locators. Three public Zomato outlet/menu observations supplement Indori poha/jalebi, Ram Ladoo and shawarma. No user reviews or ratings were republished. Google links are published business links or search links, not proof of independently verified Google listings.

A branch menu is attached only to its own outlet. A national menu or online product price remains a brand reference, with branch availability and price unconfirmed. Missing prices, portions and dietary evidence stay unknown. Published FSSAI numbers remain unverified. All source records remain excluded from owner-verified recommendations and paid eligibility.

These records are served by the existing read-only source directory and `/api/dish-outlets`. They do not modify production owner, payment or recommendation tables. Source coverage is partial and must not be presented as an all-India census.
