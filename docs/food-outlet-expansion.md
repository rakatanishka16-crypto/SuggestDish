# Official bakery and outlet expansion — 5 October 2026

This collection extends the Mumbai source catalog with nationwide Theobroma branches and Mumbai-wide Le15 delivery products. It is not a complete census of Indian or worldwide food outlets.

Published additions: 329 profiles, 384 priced menu variants and 327 source map pins. Combined catalog: 402 profiles, 896 menu entries and 632 published prices. All 328 Theobroma branch pages were checked. The 84-test suite passes.

Sources: Theobroma's public outlet directory, individual linked branch pages and 21 food menu categories; Le15's Mumbai-wide collection, 21 product pages and published contact address. Source URLs and retrieval dates are retained on every record.

Theobroma map coordinates are published only when directory and branch pins agree and lie within the India bounds used for this collection. Invalid or conflicting pins remain unknown. Published directory hours can conflict with branch-page default hours; the interface states that conflict rather than asserting current opening status. Listed delivery/pickup modes are source declarations, not confirmed current availability.

Le15 product sizes and eggless/regular variants retain individual prices. Regular variants remain of unknown dietary type unless the source establishes it. Out-of-stock source offers are retained with that status. Contact/production addresses are labelled clearly; walk-in and dine-in service are not assumed.

Identical Theobroma observations are merged; differently priced or labelled variants remain separate. Product names, price amounts and source dietary markers are recorded without copying photographs, ratings, reviews or descriptive marketing text. Conflicting ingredient evidence leaves dietary type unknown. Brand menu prices are reference information and do not guarantee branch stock, tax inclusion or current prices.

The source catalog remains a read-only supplement to Neon directory records. No new production credential, schema migration or bulk Dish write is required. The recommendation engine continues using its eligible database dishes; these source menus are reference data until branch-level availability and eligibility are established.

The public directory now optionally accepts a valid lat/lon pair and reports haversine straight-line distance in kilometres. Missing pins never become zero-distance results. Browser location is requested only after a user chooses the new distance control, is not persisted by that control, and can be cleared. Map links point to the source coordinates. Straight-line distance is not a route estimate or delivery-service guarantee.

The coverage endpoint reports combined profile/menu/price counts, profiles with coordinates and the check date of each source batch. Previous research lead and individual food licence verification gaps remain visible. An FSSAI number published by a business is labelled as an unverified declaration.

Validation includes the existing suite, coordinate/request validation, kilometre calculations, missing-location behavior, dietary variant preservation and unique source IDs. Production counts, representative directory queries, menu responses and browser rendering are checked after deployment.
